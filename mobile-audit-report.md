# Mobile landscape audit: full-screen game screens (report only)

Sizes: 863x360 (Pixel 7 landscape viewport) and 740x360 (isMobile, hasTouch, DPR 2). Dev build at http://localhost:5173. Scripts and screenshots are in the scratchpad mobile-audit folder, not in the repo. No source files were changed.

Class key: BLOCKER = cannot advance/close at all; MAJOR = needs awkward scrolling, partly covered, or target <32px; MINOR = cramped or <40px; PASS = advances/closes with no finding; GATE = advance disabled until a player choice (not exercised).

## Summary of BLOCKER and MAJOR findings

BLOCKERS (both sizes):
- vote_result: CONTINUE at y~380 in a 360px non-scrolling document. Cause: VoteResult.tsx:469-471 with layout that does not fit 360px height.
- epilogue: "Read the Epilogue" at y~1037 inside a 360px fixed, overflow-hidden CinematicPlate. Cause: EpilogueScreen.tsx:265 with scene/CinematicPlate.tsx:97.

MAJOR (reachable, but awkward or <32px):
- review: Advance to Week N buried about 2200px down (EveningReview.tsx:477).
- lobbying: PROCEED WITHOUT SPENDING below the fold (LobbyingInterface.tsx:234); 22px chips.
- radio: Cancel and PREVIEW SPEECH below the fold, 30px tall (RadioAddressView.tsx:114-115).
- diary: only advance is a 15px text hint below the fold; centre tap does nothing (DiaryScreen.tsx:160).
- midpoint: option cards need scrolling inside the modal (ProjectMidpointModal.tsx:55-56).
- saveslot: More > SAVE GAME is at y~639 in a 286px scroller, 37px tall (TopBar.tsx:382).
- Global HUD: "Chancellor details" portrait is 28x28 on every gameplay screen (KaterinHUD.tsx ~L217-224).
- Tabs and small controls <32px: codex/journal tabs 25px, settings tabs 31px, notes week arrows 28px, charmap CLOSE 60x24, province close 34x26, news arrows 31x27.

End-to-end walk: New Game, SELECT slot 1, cinematic, prologue, and the week-1 map were all reached by taps. The week-1 scene pin works with a real touch (pin, then "The Oath of Office", then choices), and the inauguration scene completed. Week 2 was NOT reached: after the scene the map offers no tap path I could identify to the week-end review or diary within the bounded attempts. See the E2E section below.
