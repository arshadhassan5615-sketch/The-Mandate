Neighbour Flags — Real-World Inspired

Each design adapts a real flag tradition to Veltria's world. Nothing is copied.

Lytheira fading colonial empire (west)

1 · Iberian arms on the division

Inspired by: Portugal (unequal bicolour, arms on the division line).

Why: An old maritime empire that still wears its arms.

2 · Royal triband

Inspired by: Spain (horizontal triband with a doubled central stripe and arms toward the hoist).

Why: Pomp, gold and debt — the empire's ledger colours.

Tyvaren militarist single-party state (north)

Inspired by: Albania (single dark heraldic beast on a red field).

Why: Severe, martial, instantly legible.

Morivask republic seized by a junta in 1959 (east)

5 · The Junta Flag

Inspired by: Bulgaria and Hungary horizontal tricolours, plus the 1967 Greek junta's phoenix emblem.

Why: The state's colours with the regime's bird imposed on them.

6 · The Exile Flag (hole in the centre)

Inspired by: Hungary in 1956, when revolutionaries cut the communist emblem out of their flag.

Why: Morivask refugees in Sevagrad fly it with the junta's emblem cut out.
