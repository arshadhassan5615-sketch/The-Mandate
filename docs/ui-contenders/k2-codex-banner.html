THE JUNTA

Until 1959, a functioning parliamentary democracy — smaller than Veltria, poorer, but governed. In September 1959, General Radovan Skala seized power in a coup, executed the elected government, and began what international observers have characterised as ethnic cleansing against the Veltrian minority in its eastern provinces.

WHAT IT WANTS

Skala claims the river valley along the shared border as historically Morivaskan. His army is small but battle-hardened.
