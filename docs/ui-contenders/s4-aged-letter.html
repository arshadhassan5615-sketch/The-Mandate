PEOPLE'S COMMISSARIAT FOR EXTERNAL RELATIONS
ORYTHIAN DOMINION

On the Occasion of Your Election

The Presidium of the Orythian Dominion acknowledges your election to the Chancellery of the Republic of Veltria. We note with interest the reformist character of your mandate, which echoes certain historical developments in our own revolutionary tradition.

The Dominion has long maintained a position of strategic neutrality regarding Veltrian internal affairs, a position we intend to continue. However, we are prepared to explore areas of mutual economic interest.

Commissariat representatives will be in Ashenmere next month. We suggest arranging preliminary discussions at that time.

M. Varenko
Commissar for External Relations
Translated by the Foreign Ministry
