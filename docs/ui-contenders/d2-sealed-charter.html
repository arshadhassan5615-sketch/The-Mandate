THE REPUBLIC OF VELTRIA

Press Freedom Charter

Decree of the Chancellor · Judicial Order
— ❧ —

Whereas the Reform Committee has laid before the Chancellery a charter to enshrine the freedom of the press as a constitutional right of the people;

And whereas the independence of the press is the guardian of every other liberty of the Republic;

BE IT THEREFORE DECREED that no government may censor, license, or shut down any publication, and that this charter shall stand among the strongest protections of the press in the region.

Sealed at Ashenmere on the eighth day of January, 1963.

KATERIN ASHAVEL · CHANCELLOR
