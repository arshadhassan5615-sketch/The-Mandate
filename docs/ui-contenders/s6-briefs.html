The Republic of Veltria

A parliamentary republic on the continent of Asturien, bordered by the Gravenmark Mountains to the north, the Ondra River delta to the east, the Amber Coast and Thalassic Sea to the south, and the agricultural plains of Lytheira to the west.

Population: Approximately 4.7 million. Capital: Ashenmere City. Currency: The Veltrian Krone (₽).

Official language: Veltrian. Minority language: Dravenni (suppressed under Halvek, now constitutionally uncertain). Religion: Church of the Eternal Flame (nominally secular state).
