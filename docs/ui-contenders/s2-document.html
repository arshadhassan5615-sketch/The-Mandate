Chancellery of the Republic of Veltria

Emergency Grain Price Ceiling

Proposed by Drazen Kolvar, Central Bank of Veltria · Week 3, March 1963

Article 1: Price Ceiling

The price of milled grain is fixed at last autumn's level until the harvest is in.

Article 2: Enforcement

Mills that sell above the ceiling lose their state licence.

Expected Consequences

Bread will stay affordable through winter.

The Thornfeld farmers' union will be angered.

K. Ashavel
Chancellor
