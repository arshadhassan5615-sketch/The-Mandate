MEMORANDUM

TO: The Chancellor

FROM: Drazen Kolvar, Central Bank of Veltria

RE: Emergency Grain Price Ceiling

DATE: Week 3 · March 1963

ECONOMIC

Emergency Grain Price Ceiling

Proposed by Drazen Kolvar, Central Bank of Veltria

Week 3 · March 1963

Bread prices in Ashenmere have risen for six weeks running. This decree fixes the price of milled grain at last autumn's level until the harvest is in.

Mills that sell above the ceiling lose their state licence.

  • → Bread will stay affordable through winter.
  • → The Thornfeld farmers' union will be angered.
