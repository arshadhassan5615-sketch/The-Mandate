DECREE 14/1963 — PRESS FREEDOM CHARTER

Judicial order · Fiscal note: no cost to the treasury

1. PURPOSE. To protect the independence of the press under the Constitution and to prohibit censorship by government.

2. PROVISIONS. No government may censor, license, or shut down any publication. The charter enshrines press freedom as a constitutional right and would be among the strongest press protections in the region.

3. ENTRY INTO FORCE. On publication in the Official Gazette.
