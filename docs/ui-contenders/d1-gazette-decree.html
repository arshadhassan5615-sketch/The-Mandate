REPUBLIC OF VELTRIA
Office of the Chancellor
DECREE No. 14 OF 1963

Press Freedom Charter

A decree of the Judicial order, on the proposal of the Reform Committee

The Chancellor of the Republic of Veltria, having regard to the Constitution of 1912, on the proposal of the Reform Committee, and having heard the Council of Ministers,

HEREBY DECREES
ARTICLE 1The independence of the press is protected under the Constitution, and censorship by any organ of government is prohibited.
ARTICLE 2No government may censor, license, or shut down any publication. Press freedom is enshrined as a constitutional right of every citizen of the Republic.
ARTICLE 3This decree enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette. It carries no cost to the national treasury.

Given at Ashenmere, this eighth day of January, in the year nineteen hundred and sixty-three.

V. Reznik
COUNTERSIGNED
CHIEF OF STAFF
KATERIN ASHAVEL
CHANCELLOR OF THE REPUBLIC
