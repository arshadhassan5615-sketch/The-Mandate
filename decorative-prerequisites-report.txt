=== SCOPE CHECK: scene-level "prerequisites" arrays and whether they ever run ===

Ground truth (read from the engine, not assumed):
  - scene.prerequisites is evaluated ONLY inside narrativeStore.ts loadScene().
  - loadScene() is called ONLY from weekStore.ts, ONLY with an agenda-sourced
    sceneId (static agenda/*.json OR a weekStore.ts hardcoded pool literal).
  - nextNodeId (processChoice) and chainTrigger.targetNodeId (chainStore) both
    call getScene() directly — bypassing loadScene() and scene.prerequisites
    ENTIRELY, unconditionally, regardless of an exclusive array. This is the
    opposite of "chain/nextNodeId respects the exclusive fallback" — it never
    even looks at prerequisites or exclusive.
  - Even when loadScene() DOES evaluate prerequisites and they fail, the check
    only has an effect if scene.exclusive has an alternative that passes; with
    no exclusive (or none valid), the failure is silently ignored and the scene
    renders anyway.
